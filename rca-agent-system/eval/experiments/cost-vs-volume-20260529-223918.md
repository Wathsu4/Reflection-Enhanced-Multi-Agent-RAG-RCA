# Cost-vs-volume simulation (gate) — RQ4 / E6.3

Stream of **1000** chunks at **5.0%** incident rate (**50** incidents), **4** Gemini calls per RCA. No Gemini calls were made -- this is a deterministic simulation.

## Headline

- Gemini calls **gated** (our system): **200**
- Gemini calls **ungated** (RCA on every chunk): **4000**
- **Reduction: 20.0x fewer Gemini calls** (3800 calls saved)
- Classifier inferences (cheap, ~50 ms each): 1000

## Wall-time model

| Policy | classifier | RCA runs | est. wall time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gated (ours) | 1000 chunks | 50 | 1450.0 s |
| Ungated | — | 1000 | 28000.0 s |

Estimated **19.31x** wall-time speedup at ~28s per RCA.

## Why this defends RQ4

Gated compute scales with the incident count, not the log volume: the 20.0x Gemini-call reduction equals the volume-to-incident ratio (100 / incident_pct). The classifier gate is itself cheap and accurate (see the classifier-metrics card: macro F1 ~0.95 on the held-out test split), so it rarely wastes an RCA on a non-incident or misses a real one.
